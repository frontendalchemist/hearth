Health Hub keeps the medical side of caregiving organized: who the doctors
are, what's being prescribed, what's overdue.

## What it will cover

### Sprint 10 — Medications

- Medication list with RxNorm autocomplete
- Beers Criteria flagging (medications generally inappropriate for older
  adults)
- Drug interaction checks via OpenFDA
- Refill reminders

### Sprint 11 — Screenings, providers, appointments

- USPSTF-aligned screening schedule with next-due dates
- Provider directory with specialty, contact info, and primary-provider
  designation
- Specialist recommendations (e.g. "geriatrician recommended for age 78+")
- Upcoming appointments with prep checklists
