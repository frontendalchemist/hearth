## Is Hearth a substitute for medical advice?

No. Hearth is a coordination tool — it helps families track and share
information about caregiving. It does not provide medical, legal, or
financial advice, and should not be used in place of a clinician or
attorney.

## Who can see my family's data?

Only people you invite to a specific care recipient's record. Each
recipient is a separate workspace; being invited to your mother's
record doesn't grant access to your father's. Family members have
roles (Admin / Coordinator / Viewer) that scope what they can do.

Hearth staff cannot read uploaded document contents — files are stored
encrypted in S3 and the database holds metadata only.

## How does Hearth handle HIPAA?

Hearth is HIPAA-adjacent: built for personal-health data, with the
controls a covered entity would expect (access scoping per care
recipient, audit logs retained 7 years, encryption at rest and in
transit, no third-party analytics on PHI), but the app itself is not
a covered entity unless integrated by one. The full Notice of Privacy
Practices is in the in-product Privacy Policy.

## How do I delete my account / data?

From your account settings: **Delete account**. This hard-deletes
your profile, care recipient records you administer, and document
metadata; S3 objects are removed; audit log entries are anonymized
(not removed — HIPAA requires a 7-year retention).
