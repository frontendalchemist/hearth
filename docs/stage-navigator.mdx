Stage Navigator helps families understand where their loved one is in the
arc of aging and what to expect next — without forcing a single linear
"declining" framing. It uses a two-track model: independent functioning
(physical, ADL/IADL) and cognitive function (CDR-aligned) move
separately.

## What it will cover

- Stage progress for both tracks, side by side
- Warning Signs Log across five domains: cognitive, physical,
  functional, financial, social — with system-assigned urgency
  (MONITOR / SEE_DOCTOR / ACT_NOW)
- "Normal aging vs. concern" comparison grid for common observations
- Conversation guides for hard talks (driving, finances, moving)
- Stage transition history — when a change was assessed, by whom,
  what notes were captured
