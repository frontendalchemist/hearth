The Family Vault is where a household stores the paperwork that's hard to
find at the moment you actually need it: powers of attorney, advance
directives, insurance cards, care-plan agreements, recent labs.

## What it will cover

- Uploading documents (drag-and-drop or photo capture from a phone)
- AI-assisted categorization (LEGAL / MEDICAL / FINANCIAL / INSURANCE / IDENTITY / CARE_PLANS)
- Custom categories when the presets don't fit
- Per-document visibility (all members, admins only, specific members)
- Sharing — emergency packets, single-document share links, and care
  summaries — with PIN protection, view limits, and expiry
- Expiry reminders (insurance cards renewing, advance directives reaching
  the recommended 5-year refresh)
- "Remind Dorothy" — sending a non-Hearth contact a request to upload a
  missing document
