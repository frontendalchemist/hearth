# Getting Started

Welcome to Hearth — a secure place to coordinate care for your aging loved ones.

## Create your account

1. Go to [hearth.app](https://hearth.app) and click **Sign in**
2. Choose **Create one** to register with your email address
3. Verify your email and complete the setup wizard

## Add a care recipient

A *care recipient* is the person you're caring for (e.g., a parent or grandparent).

1. After signing in, click **Add care recipient**
2. Enter their name and date of birth
3. Select their current care stage — don't worry, you can update this anytime

## Invite family members

You can invite other family members to collaborate on a care recipient's profile.

1. Open the care recipient's profile
2. Click **Invite family member**
3. Enter their email address and choose their role:
   - **Admin** — can upload, edit, and delete documents and tasks
   - **Member** — can upload documents and add care log entries
   - **Viewer** — read-only access
